COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you buy

Can't find your answer? Ask us →

Why is printing a plan, and do I need one?

Yes, you need one: the module is a one-time purchase, and printing runs on a VentorTech printing plan, billed monthly or yearly. Without a plan the module cannot print.

Every document travels through our print service, which runs around the clock, and the plan pays for it. That keeps the work off your Odoo — it hands the document over and moves on — and covers fixes, improvements and support from the team that builds it.

The first 90 days are included. Plans and prices: go.ventor.tech/dp-plans

What happens when the 90 days are over?

Your plan starts and the card you added is charged for the first time. Cancel before then and nothing is charged.

Do I need an IoT Box or a print server?

No. Our app runs on a Windows, macOS or Linux computer, or a Raspberry Pi, that already prints to your printers. That computer is the only thing needed on your side.

Which printers and scales work with it?

Any printer that computer already prints to — the rule is that simple. Office laser and inkjet printers, label printers such as Zebra in PDF or ZPL, connected by USB, network, Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. Scales connect to the same computer and send the weight to Odoo.

Where do my documents go? Are they stored?

From your Odoo, through our print service, to the computer next to the printer. The service holds a document only until that computer takes it, which is usually a second or two, and does not keep it once it has printed; nobody reads it. If the computer is switched off, the job waits for it for up to 14 days and prints when it is back.

With Secure Printing turned on, only a short-lived link travels: the computer downloads the document from Odoo itself, so if Odoo is reachable only inside your network, nobody outside it can open the link.

Does it work on Odoo.sh and Odoo Online?

Odoo.sh, your own server and any other cloud: yes. Odoo Online does not allow third-party modules, so it cannot be installed there.

We are moving to a new Odoo version. What do we buy?

Only the module for the new version. Your printing plan carries on unchanged.
